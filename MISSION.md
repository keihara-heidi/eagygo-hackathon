# Mission: Win the KICK "Chat Insights & Engagement" hackathon challenge

## Why
The team is at the EasyGo hackathon today and must choose a prototype direction for Challenge 02, build it, and defend it in a 5-minute presentation. A well-chosen, well-justified direction is the difference between a demo that is obvious in two minutes and six hours spent on something the judges read as a one-way dashboard.

## Success looks like
- Pick one prototype direction and articulate why it beats the alternatives against the judging criteria.
- Explain any of the candidate directions — its feedback loop, its data signals, its risks — in under two minutes.
- Keep the mock data faithful to the real KICK Public API payloads so the prototype is credible.

## Constraints
- Submission deadline 4:00 PM today (Saturday Aug 1, 2026); only committed code counts.
- Roughly 6 hours of build time remain from 9:45 AM.
- Deliverables: 5-minute solution presentation + working prototype (mocked data allowed).
- User prefers brief, decision-oriented explanations.

## Out of scope
- The Wild Card challenge, until the main challenge is demoable.
- Real OAuth, real webhook deployment, or a live KICK channel — mocks only today.
- Production hardening, auth, multi-channel support.
