# Challenge 2: Chat Insights / Engagement

**Your mission is to answer... How would you turn chat into something more than a scroll of messages?**

## The Pitch

Chat is where a stream actually happens, but most of what’s said in it disappears the second it scrolls past. Streamers can’t always see what’s landing, and viewers don’t always have a way to be more than “just another message.”

**What if chat could talk back?** That’s the idea behind this challenge: build something that either helps a streamer understand their chat better, or gives viewers a new way to participate in it, beyond typing and hoping someone notices.

## The Challenge

Keep this simple and open. There’s no single right direction; insights and engagement are just a starting point. Use the KICK Public API docs as a guide for what’s possible with real chat data, and feel free to mock any data you need.

## Think about

- [ ] Would you build for the **streamer**—insights such as what chat is feeling, what’s trending, and who’s showing up—or for the **viewer**—engagement such as polls, predictions, reactions, or mini-games? Either direction is fair game.
- [ ] What would the feedback loop look like—streamer sees something, and viewers see something back?
- [ ] What would you pull from chat to make this work—message volume, sentiment, keywords, emotes, or specific commands?
- [ ] How do you keep it simple enough that it adds to the stream instead of getting in the way?

## What Good Looks Like

A strong submission makes the idea obvious in under two minutes.

The best teams will show:

- A clear, simple idea that’s easy to explain in under two minutes.
- A genuine feedback loop between streamer and viewer.
- A prototype that feels usable, not just a concept deck.

## What You Build

### 1. Solution Presentation — 5 Minutes

A presentation explaining your idea. It should cover:

- What your idea is, and whether it is insights-focused, engagement-focused, or a bit of both.
- What data or signals from chat it uses.
- What the streamer and/or viewer experience looks like.
- Why it creates a genuine back-and-forth, not just a one-way display.

### 2. Working Prototype

Build a clickable or demo-able prototype using mocked or dummy data, as long as the mock reflects real data available on KICK.

It should show:

- Your idea in action—a dashboard, overlay, chat-side feature, or whatever fits best.
- The core interaction or insight your idea produces.
- The feedback loop between streamer and viewer, however simple.

## Wild Card

On top of your main challenge, every team also takes on the Wild Card Challenge.

There are no limits here. Build anything you want. Use the KICK Public API docs as inspiration if it helps, or mock whatever data you need. There’s no fixed brief and no required feature list—just something creative that shows off a different side of your team.

Keep it as simple or as ambitious as you like. Present it the same way as your main challenge, with a presentation page and a working prototype, if you have the time.
